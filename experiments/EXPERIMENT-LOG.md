## Planned experiments

### EXP-20261028-001 — Skin texture wording
- **Status:** planned.
- **Question:** Does restrained skin-texture wording improve naturalness without introducing repeated stippling?
- **Variable:** skin-texture module.
- **Conditions:** baseline / restrained / detailed.
- **Scenario:** natural portrait under a single large soft source.
- **Model:** select and record before generation.
- **Evidence leads:** Skin Realism Pack, CHI 2025 photorealism study, facial texture research in SOURCES.md.
- **Success criterion:** improved blind naturalness ratings without a material increase in stippling or waxy artifacts.

### EXP-20261028-002 — Light-source geometry
- **Status:** planned.
- **Question:** Does naming source size and direction improve shadow and highlight coherence?
- **Variable:** lighting module.
- **Conditions:** generic soft light / large source and direction / source plus fill and shadow transitions.
- **Scenario:** close portrait with consistent framing.
- **Success criterion:** improved shadow-direction and catchlight ratings across multiple samples.

### EXP-20261028-003 — Camera scenario versus focal length alone
- **Status:** planned.
- **Question:** Does a coherent capture description improve field of view, focus, and scene consistency?
- **Variable:** camera description.
- **Conditions:** focal length only / focal length plus distance / complete camera and focus target.
- **Scenario:** environmental portrait.
- **Evidence leads:** Generative Photography, PreciseCam, CamEdit.
- **Success criterion:** measured consistency with requested framing and focus, not simply a more attractive image.
