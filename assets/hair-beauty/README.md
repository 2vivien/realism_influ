# Hair & Facial Beauty Pack

**Status:** initial taxonomy; examples require model-specific testing.

The goal is believable human detail and grooming without forcing every face into one beauty standard.

## Hair
Track hairline shape and density, individual strands versus clumps, flyaways, curl structure, strand direction, highlights, occlusion at ears/forehead/neck, movement, focus, and motion.

Prompt fragment:  
“Hair forms coherent strand groups with a few naturally irregular flyaways, varied strand highlights following the key light, and plausible occlusion at the hairline and around the ears; avoid helmet-like masses and repeated filament patterns.”

## Eyes, brows, and lashes
Track eyelid geometry, iris/pupil/sclera, gaze direction, catchlights, brow density and hair direction, lash roots, and focus. Catchlights should match plausible light sources. Avoid glassy eyes, oversized catchlights, identical lashes, and excessively sharp iris rings.

## Lips, teeth, and expression
Track lip texture and moisture, lipstick boundaries, teeth visible in a way supported by the expression, and coordinated movement around eyes, cheeks, and mouth. Avoid uniform bright tooth blocks or mechanically mirrored smiles.

## Makeup and grooming
Describe makeup style and coverage, visible skin through makeup where appropriate, blending, hairstyle, and consistency across face, neck, and ears.

## Experiment axes
1. Hair detail: grouped strands versus explicit individual strands.
2. Flyaways: none / subtle / visible in backlight.
3. Catchlights: unspecified / one plausible source / multiple motivated sources.
4. Makeup: none / natural / editorial.
5. Expression: neutral / subtle smile / active expression.
6. Retouching: documentary / restrained beauty / high-end editorial.

Change one major axis per test. Hair and eye detail depend on crop, resolution, focus, and lighting; record these conditions.
