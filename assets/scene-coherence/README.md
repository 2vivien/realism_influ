# Scene Coherence & Composition Pack

Photographic plausibility depends on the entire frame, not only the face.

## Anatomy and pose
Check joint locations, limb continuity, hand-object contact, body weight, support surfaces, facial expression coordination, and plausible asymmetry without distortion.

## Light transport
Shadows must agree with source direction and object placement. Contact shadows belong where objects touch or nearly touch surfaces. Reflections must fit material, geometry, and environment. Transparent objects need plausible refraction/reflection. Skin, fabric, metal, glass, and matte materials should not share identical specular response.

## Perspective and geometry
Objects on the same plane should have compatible perspective. Background architecture, scale, and occlusion should be coherent. Lens distortion must affect the frame plausibly. Avoid contradictory wide-angle and compressed-telephoto cues unless a specific setup explains them.

## Composition and capture intent
- **Casual selfie:** camera height, arm distance, and framing fit the scenario.
- **Friend-taken snapshot:** framing may be imperfect but camera position remains plausible.
- **Documentary scene:** foreground, subject, and background share capture conditions.
- **Editorial portrait:** controlled lighting and deliberate composition are compatible with realism.
- **Product photograph:** material rendering, contact shadow, scale, and reflections are critical.

## Scenario cards
- **SCN-001 Casual indoor smartphone portrait:** who holds the camera, distance, room illumination, and expected phone processing?
- **SCN-002 Friend-taken outdoor candid:** daylight direction, movement, framing, background, and focus?
- **SCN-003 Window-lit beauty portrait:** source size, face angle, fill, catchlights, skin highlights, and retouching?
- **SCN-004 Warm restaurant interior:** mixed lights, background exposure, glass reflections, and local color?
- **SCN-005 Fashion editorial:** lens/distance, styling, key/fill/rim, fabric, and post-production?
- **SCN-006 Product in environment:** contact, scale, reflections, shadows, material roughness, and perspective?

## Coherence prompt module

“Maintain one consistent photographic setup across the entire frame: perspective, focus, light direction, shadow softness, reflections, material response, and color rendering agree with the stated camera position and environment.”

## Review order

1. Global geometry and composition.
2. Light direction and shadow logic.
3. Anatomy and object interaction.
4. Material response and reflections.
5. Focus and depth of field.
6. Color and processing.
7. Small artifacts and texture.

Fix large contradictions before adding microtexture or grain.
